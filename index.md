# Second Brain OS

A personal knowledge operating system built on three open frameworks — for anyone who wants their notes, daily life, and AI tools to actually work together.

**This is not an app.** It's a pattern. A folder structure, a set of conventions, and a small CLI tool that ties it together. It runs on your machine, syncs through iCloud or any file system, and works with any AI — local or cloud.

---

## The Three Frameworks

### 🗂️ AIOS — Your Orientation Layer
Every vault needs a control plane: who you are, how the vault is structured, what tools can do on your behalf. AIOS is that layer — a small folder of markdown files that orients any AI or tool to your system without exposing sensitive data.

→ [Learn about AIOS](aios)

---

### 🧠 The Wiki — Your Knowledge Base
A structured, AI-assisted knowledge base organized into three buckets: **Atlas** (what you know), **Calendar** (what happened), **Efforts** (what you're working on). Drop files into an inbox and a local AI routes and ingests them for you.

→ [Learn about the Wiki](wiki)

---

### 📓 ForeverNote — Your Daily Container
One file per calendar date. Every year stacks inside it. Your morning briefing, meetings, journal entries, and habits all live in one place per day — built up incrementally throughout the day, not assembled all at once.

→ [Learn about ForeverNote](forevernote)

---

## Philosophy

- **Tool-agnostic** — works with Claude, GPT, Gemini, or a fully local model. Swap the AI without rebuilding the system.
- **Local-first optional** — the reference implementation runs on [Ollama](https://ollama.ai) with no data leaving your machine. Cloud APIs work too.
- **The vault owns the data** — plain markdown files. No proprietary format, no lock-in. Obsidian, VS Code, or a text editor all work.
- **AI as a tool, not a dependency** — the vault is useful without AI. AI makes it faster.
