# Atlas — Schema

**Purpose:** Permanent knowledge. Things that don't expire — they just get richer.

**Route here when:** Content is a concept, person, domain, technology, decision, or reference material with no expiry date.

**Route to calendar instead when:** Content is tied to a specific date or event.  
**Route to efforts instead when:** Content is an active project or goal.

## Note Types

- `people/name.md` — a person you interact with
- `concepts/topic.md` — an idea, framework, or technology
- `domains/area.md` — a field or area of knowledge
- `decisions/decision.md` — a significant decision and its rationale
